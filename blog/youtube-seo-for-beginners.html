YouTube SEO for Beginners: A Simple 6-Step Guide

By Viralora · Updated September 2026

YouTube is the second largest search engine, and most beginners ignore search completely. You do not need expensive tools to rank. You need to help YouTube understand what your video is about and who should watch it.

1. Start with keyword research

Type your topic in the YouTube search bar and read the autocomplete suggestions. These are real searches. Pick a phrase with a clear intent, such as "how to edit videos on phone", instead of a broad word like "editing".

2. Optimise your title and description

Use the main keyword in the title, and write the first two lines of the description as a natural summary that also contains it. Add a few paragraphs explaining the video, plus useful links and timestamps. Avoid stuffing the same word again and again.

3. Use tags and hashtags lightly

Tags matter far less than they used to. Add a handful that describe the topic and common misspellings, and use two or three relevant hashtags. Time spent on the title and thumbnail pays off more.

4. Hook viewers in the first 15 seconds

Watch time and audience retention are strong signals. Open with the result or the biggest promise of the video, not a long intro. Cut pauses and repeated points during editing.

5. Make the thumbnail work with the title

The thumbnail and title should tell one story together. Use big readable text, a clear face or object, and high contrast. Do not repeat the exact title words on the image.

6. Be consistent and study your analytics

Upload on a schedule you can keep. In YouTube Studio, watch which videos bring new viewers and where people stop watching, then make more of what works.
