How to Write YouTube Titles That Get Clicks

By Viralora · Updated September 2026

Your title is the first thing a viewer judges, often before they even notice your thumbnail. A strong title can double your click-through rate without changing the video at all. Here is a practical way to write better ones.

1. Promise one clear outcome

Viewers click when they know exactly what they will get. Compare "My Budget" with "How I Saved 50,000 Rupees in 3 Months". The second names a result and a timeframe. Ask yourself: what will the viewer know or be able to do after watching?

2. Create curiosity without lying

Curiosity gaps work when the video really answers the question. Use phrases like "the mistake most beginners make" only if you reveal that mistake. Misleading titles raise clicks briefly, then hurt watch time, and YouTube then shows the video to fewer people.

3. Put the keyword early

People search with specific words. Place the main keyword in the first few words, for example "YouTube SEO for beginners: 5 steps". Titles get cut off on phones after roughly 60 characters, so keep the important part at the start.

4. Use numbers and specifics

  • "7 Editing Tricks" feels more concrete than "Editing Tricks".
  • Odd numbers and exact figures tend to feel more believable.
  • Use real numbers from your own experience.

5. Test and learn

Write five versions of every title, then pick the one that is clear and honest. After a week, check impressions click-through rate in YouTube Studio. If a video gets impressions but few clicks, change the title or thumbnail and compare again.
