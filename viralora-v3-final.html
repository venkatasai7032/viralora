Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026 · Applies to viralora.org

🔒 Short version: Viralora does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. All AI tools run entirely in your browser using smart JavaScript. We use Google AdSense for ads.

1. Who We Are

Viralora (viralora.org) is operated by Venkata Sai Yadav, based in Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, India. We provide browser-based AI content generation tools for creators worldwide.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone)
  • Account data — no sign-up or login exists
  • Your prompts or outputs — all runs locally in your browser
  • Payment information — all tools are completely free

3. How Our AI Tools Work

All AI tools use a smart JavaScript template engine that runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Everything stays on your device.
